Charging stations come from Google Places nearby search along each route. Road quality is estimated from average driving speed and route elevation change — Google Maps does not expose a direct road-condition signal, so this is a heuristic, not a pothole report. Suggested stop spacing is based on the range from your last Calculator session (or a default assumption if none is saved), with a buffer kept in reserve. Weather is current conditions at the start and end points, not a forecast along the whole route.
Trip planning uses your browser's location only with permission, and is not stored anywhere.
